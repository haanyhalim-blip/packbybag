Ready-made lists & common questions
  
Ready-made packing lists

  
  • Family holiday with kids – Sun, pool or beach, a week or two. Everything for the grown-ups plus the kids' bag and the get-there shop.
  • Holiday with your partner – A week away for two. Lighter than the family list, with a nice evening or two built in.
  • Work / business trip – Two to four nights, meetings and a hotel. Laptop in hand luggage, shirts in the case, nothing forgotten in the room.
  • Going to an event – A conference, exhibition or a few days of meetings. Long days on your feet, lots of people, business cards at the ready.
  • Long-haul, hand luggage only – One cabin bag, no waiting at the carousel. Everything goes in hand luggage – liquids under 100 ml.

  
School bag lists by age

  
  • Nursery & pre-school – Ages 2–4. Spare clothes, comforter, outdoor gear and the nappy bits if they still need them.
  • Infants (Reception – Year 2) – Ages 4–7. Book bag and reading record, PE kit on PE days, spare pants for the little ones.
  • Juniors (Years 3 – 6) – Ages 7–11. Pencil case, homework and spellings, swim and club days – and keys or a phone for Year 6.
  • Secondary (Years 7 – 11) – Ages 11–16, and a good start for sixth form. Planner, today's books, calculator, bus pass, charged phone.

  
Questions

  
How does Pack by Bag work?

  
Type or paste what you're taking, or start from a ready-made list. Everything is sorted into bags – hand luggage, suitcase, wear on the day, kids' bags, to-dos before you go and things to buy when you get there – so you pack one bag at a time and tick things off as they go in.

  
What should go in hand luggage rather than the suitcase?

  
Passports and travel documents, bank cards, phone and chargers, medicines, glasses, anything valuable and a change of clothes in case the case goes missing. Liquids in hand luggage usually need to be under 100 ml each – check your airport. Pack by Bag puts these in hand luggage for you, and you can move anything with one tap.

  
What should my child take to school?

  
It depends on their age. Nursery children need spare clothes, a comforter and outdoor gear. Infants (Reception to Year 2) need a book bag, reading record, water bottle and PE kit on PE days. Juniors (Years 3 to 6) add a pencil case, homework, spellings and swim or club kit. Secondary pupils need a planner, today's exercise books, a scientific calculator, a bus pass and a charged phone. Pick the list for their age, add each child's name, and every child gets their own section.

  
Can I use the same school list every day?

  
Yes. Tap Untick all and the list stays, ready to pack again tomorrow morning.

  
Can two people pack from the same list?

  
Yes. Share a link and you both see the same list, ticked off as you go. Or send it as a message by WhatsApp or email.

  
Is it free?

  
Yes – free, with no account and no sign-up. Your list stays on your phone unless you share it.

Why PackbyBag?

  
A packing list sorted by bag – hand luggage, suitcase, what you wear on the day, the kids' bags – plus a to-do list for before you go. Pack one bag at a time and nothing gets left on the bed.

  
Free, no account. Share the link and you both pack from the same list.

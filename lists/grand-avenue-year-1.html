Grand Avenue – Year 1

  • School shoes

School bag

  • Lunchbox

To do before you go

  • Sign the reading record
  • Read together
  • Check the school app / emails
  • Lay out uniform the night before
  • Pack the bag the night before
  • Label everything with their name
  • Wash the PE kit
  • Put sun cream onif needed

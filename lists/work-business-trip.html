Work / business trip

Two to four nights, meetings and a hotel. Laptop in hand luggage, shirts in the case, nothing forgotten in the room.

Use this list in PackbyBag →

Send on WhatsAppPrint

Hand luggage – keep it with you

  • Passport
  • Driving licence
  • Boarding passes
  • Train / flight tickets
  • Hotel booking
  • Travel insurance details
  • Bank cards
  • Cash (local currency)
  • Work ID / badge
  • House keys
  • Pen (for forms)
  • Phone
  • Phone charger
  • Power bank
  • Plug adapter
  • Laptop
  • Laptop charger
  • Headphones
  • Portable mouse
  • USB cable
  • Laptop bag
  • Notebook
  • Pens
  • Business cards
  • Presentation on USB
  • Receipts envelope (expenses)
  • Toothbrush
  • Toothpaste
  • Moisturiser
  • Travel-size toiletries (under 100 ml)
  • Prescription medicines
  • Paracetamol
  • Plasters
  • Reusable water bottle (empty through security)
  • Book
  • Downloaded playlist / podcasts
  • Cabin case

Suitcase – hold luggage

  • Underwear
  • Socks
  • Suit
  • Ironed shirts
  • Ties
  • Smart outfit
  • Pyjamas
  • Gym kit
  • Smart shoes
  • Deodorant
  • Shampoo
  • Shower gel
  • Razor
  • Shaving foam
  • Hairbrush
  • Perfume / aftershave
  • Wash bag
  • Luggage tags

Wear on the day

  • Belt
  • Jacket
  • Trainers

To do before you go

  • Check in online
  • Download boarding passes
  • Set an out-of-office
  • Charge everything
  • Save bookings to your phone
  • Book airport parking / transfer
  • Register the trip with the office
